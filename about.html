Spider SEO

Chrome companion ·

Inspect the SEO, analytics, consent of pages you choose in your own Chrome session.

Visit spider-seo.app · Privacy & data controls · Open inspector

The extension works on its own. You can optionally pair it with the Spider SEO macOS app or MCP using an expiring code. Only explicitly requested pages are tested. App/MCP results identify when the Chrome extension is used.

Permissions

  • Active tab and scripting: inspect the page you choose.
  • Optional HTTP(S) site access: requested per site for rendered content and known analytics provider requests.
  • Web requests and navigation: observe supported events and resume capture on reload, only on recording tabs.
  • Storage: session-only results and local pairing credentials. Alarms: check the paired app/MCP connection.
  • Optional loopback access: authenticated communication with Spider SEO on 127.0.0.1 only.

No ads, developer telemetry, browsing-history collection, cookie reading, or remote executable code. The extension does not change cookie consent. It does not require a Spider SEO Pro subscription for its standalone inspection features.
